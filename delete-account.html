Delete your BizTrack account

Last updated: 29 October 2026

You can delete your BizTrack account and all its data at any time. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open BizTrack and log in.
  2. Open the menu and tap your name at the bottom to open Your profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Optionally tap Export my data first to keep a copy.
  5. Enter your password, type DELETE, and tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't log in? Ask us by email

Email orallan19@gmail.com from the email address of your BizTrack account, with the subject "Delete my BizTrack account". We will confirm the request and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name and login).
  • Your profile (username, photo, phone, town and description).
  • All your business records stored online, and the copy on the device you delete from.
  • Your plan, trial and payment records held by BizTrack.

What may be kept

  • Our payment provider (Relworx) and your mobile network (MTN or Airtel) keep their own records of payments you made, as the law requires. BizTrack does not control those records.
  • If you used BizTrack on other phones or computers, the encrypted copy on those devices is removed the next time you open BizTrack on them and log out choosing to remove the device's data, or when you uninstall the app or clear the browser's data.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle your data.
