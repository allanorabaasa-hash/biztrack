BizTrack

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

BizTrack is a business accounting app for small businesses. It is run by Allan Orabaasa in Uganda ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information BizTrack handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the BizTrack web app and the BizTrack Android app.

In short: your business records are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so we cannot read them. We keep only what we need to run your account and take payments. We do not sell your information or show ads.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and password. Your password is handled by our login provider and is never stored by us in readable form.
  • Your name.

Profile information (optional)

  • A username, profile photo, phone number, town or city, and a short description, if you choose to add them.

Your business records (encrypted)

  • The businesses, transactions, sales, invoices, customers, products and stock you enter.
  • These are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your password (and by your recovery code) before they are saved online. We store only the encrypted version and cannot read it.

Plan and payment information

  • Your plan, free-trial dates and plan expiry date.
  • For each payment: the mobile money number you paid from, the amount, the plan, the date and status, and the reference numbers from our payment provider and your mobile network.
  • We never see or store your Mobile Money PIN. You enter it only on your phone, in your network's own prompt.

Information stored on your device

  • An encrypted copy of your business records and the key to open it, so the app works offline.
  • Small settings, such as your selected business, the phone number you last paid with, a copy of your profile, and the time you last used the app (so you stay signed in for up to 30 minutes when you switch away).

BizTrack does not use advertising or third-party analytics, does not track your location, and does not access your contacts, camera roll or files except a photo you choose for your profile.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store and sync your encrypted records across your devices.
  • To run your free trial and paid plans, take payments and confirm them with our payment provider.
  • To send emails you need, such as account confirmation and password reset.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal and tax duties.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run BizTrack:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions. Your data is stored on their servers.
  • Relworx, our payment provider, and your mobile network (MTN or Airtel) process mobile money payments. They receive your phone number and the payment amount.
  • GitHub Pages serves the web app's files.

The News section (Premium) shows public business news. Our server fetches it from public news websites; no personal information is sent to them.

We may disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell your information.

4. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account, profile and encrypted records until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, encrypted records and payment records from BizTrack. Our payment provider and mobile networks may keep their own transaction records as the law requires.
  • Data stored on your device is removed when you delete your account in the app, or when you log out and choose to remove this device's data.

5. Security

Business records are encrypted on your device using AES-256 before upload. Connections use HTTPS. Access to each account's data is restricted by database security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your recovery code private.

Because of this encryption, if you lose both your password and your recovery code, nobody, including us, can unlock your records.

6. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy (you can export your records from the app at any time);
  • correct your information (edit your profile in the app);
  • have your information deleted (see Delete your account);
  • object to how we use your information.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) of Uganda.

7. Children

BizTrack is meant for people aged 18 and over who run or work in a business. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
